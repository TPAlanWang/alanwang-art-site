關於/ About

往返於台北與台中的爵士小號演奏者。
深受 Clifford Brown, Fabrizio Bosso 影響，演奏風格融合了傳統 Hard Bop 的律動與現代即興的色彩。
在舞台上湧動奔流的旋律，與隨煙霧飄揚的茶香之間，尋找流動的平靜；
在標準曲的曲式與嚴謹的服裝版形結構中，探索自由穿梭的路徑。

A jazz trumpeter moving between Taipei and Taichung.
Deeply influenced by Clifford Brown and Fabrizio Bosso,
the playing blends the rhythmic drive of traditional hard bop with the colors of modern improvisation.
Between the surging flow of melody on stage and the drifting aroma of tea carried in the air,
there is a search for a calm in motion—
and within the forms of jazz standards and the rigor of tailored garment structures,
an exploration of paths through which freedom can quietly pass.

重要經歷 Selected Experience

  • 臺北爵士大樂隊（Taipei Jazz Orchestra） 團員。
    Member of the Taipei Jazz Orchestra.

  • 參與 兩廳院夏日爵士派對，於國家音樂廳演出。
    Performed at the NTCH Summer Jazz in the National Concert Hall.

  • 甜約翰（Sweet John） 新專輯《GOOD AFTERNIGHT》巡迴演出小號手。
    Trumpeter for Sweet John's "GOOD AFTERNIGHT" Album Tour.

  • 2019 台中爵士音樂節 及 2017 台北爵士音樂節 比賽雙冠軍。
    1st Place Winner of both Taichung Jazz Festival (2019) and Taipei Jazz Festival (2017).

  • 若晞茶空間（Roche Tea Space） 茶師及策劃人（2023–2025）；策劃爵士跨界展演《樂音茶席》。
    Tea Practitioner and Curator; Produced and performed the jazz-tea crossover series "Melodic Tea Gathering".

  • 金馬獎獲獎電影《同學麥娜絲》、湖南衛視《歌手 當打之年》 及短片與多部音樂劇配樂錄製小號手。
    Trumpet Soloist for the film "Classmates Minus" and the TV show "Singer 2020".

  • 與 Bossa Nova 女王 小野麗莎（Lisa Ono） 於桃園管樂節演出。 Performed with Bossa Nova legend Lisa Ono at the Taoyuan Band Festival.

  • 具備 新加坡、馬來西亞 等地國際巡演經驗。
    Extensive international touring experience in Singapore and Malaysia.
